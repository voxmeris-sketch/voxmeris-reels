Shorts Scheduler

A private tool run by Voxmeris

Shorts Scheduler is a personal tool that uploads and schedules the creator's own YouTube Shorts on two channels the creator owns: Voxmeris (football stats) and Hoops Ranked (basketball stats). It is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.

This site also briefly hosts video files while they are published to the Voxmeris Instagram account.
